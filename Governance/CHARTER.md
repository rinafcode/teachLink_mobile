# TeachLink Mobile Project Charter

## Version

This charter is version 1.0 and is effective as of 2026-09-26.

## Purpose

TeachLink Mobile is a mobile-first learning platform and repository for the
TeachLink community. The project exists to provide a reliable, accessible, and
well-governed learning experience for students, educators, and contributors while
maintaining a respectful, transparent, and sustainable development process.

This charter defines the project's mission, authority, decision-making structure,
and the rules by which the governance documents in this repository are maintained.
It is intended to provide a clear and stable reference for maintainers,
contributors, and community members.

## Mission and Scope

The mission of TeachLink Mobile is to:

- build and maintain a useful and accessible mobile learning experience;
- support educational workflows that are reliable, secure, and user-centered;
- enable community-driven contribution, review, and iteration;
- keep project decisions transparent, documented, and traceable;
- uphold a high standard for quality, accessibility, and security.

This charter applies to the TeachLink Mobile repository and all governance,
release, security, and contribution processes linked to it. The project may evolve
through pull requests, issues, release processes, and governance decisions made
within the repository.

## Project Authority

The project is governed by the repository's maintainers and the documented
processes in the `Governance/` folder. Authority is delegated as follows:

### Maintainers

The maintainers hold decision-making authority for the project. Their authority
includes:

- setting strategic direction and technical priorities;
- approving changes to project policy and governance;
- establishing review standards and release readiness;
- resolving disputes that cannot be resolved by normal review flow;
- determining the eligibility and scope of project releases;
- acting as the final authority for repository governance when the policies in
  this folder do not yet address a matter.

Maintainers are expected to act in the best interests of the project, to make
reasoned decisions based on documented process, and to document material decisions
in issues, pull requests, or governance records.

### Contributors

Contributors hold proposal and implementation authority. They may:

- submit pull requests and issue reports;
- propose features, fixes, and governance changes;
- participate in review and discussion;
- propose policy improvements for the project.

Contributors do not hold final decision authority over project governance, but
their work and feedback are essential to the project's health and direction.

### Security and Policy Bodies

Where specific governance documents define roles or teams, those bodies hold the
authority described in their own policy documents. In particular, governance
policies in this folder may establish review, security, conflict-resolution, and
release processes that complement this charter.

## Decision-Making Principles

The project operates under the following principles:

- openness — decisions should be visible and reviewable;
- transparency — rationale should be documented where material;
- inclusion — contributors and maintainers should be able to participate in
  review and discussion;
- consensus first — the project prefers alignment through discussion and review;
- accountability — decisions are owned by the maintainers and recorded in a
  traceable way.

If a decision cannot be reached by consensus, the maintainer group may resolve it
by documented majority decision. Any decision affecting governance itself should
be made with explicit review under this charter and the governing policies in
`Governance/`.

## Scope of Governance

This charter governs the project's repository-level policy and operational
structure. It does not override any applicable external legal obligations,
licensing requirements, or obligations imposed by the hosting platform. Instead,
it provides the internal framework for how TeachLink Mobile is run and how changes
are evaluated within the project.

## Amendment Process

This charter may be amended by following the project's governance process.
Amendments must be proposed through a pull request or issue that affects only the
`Governance/` folder, consistent with the repository's governance conventions.

To amend the charter:

1. A change must be proposed in a tracked pull request or issue with a clear
   rationale.
2. The proposed amendment must be reviewed by the maintainers.
3. The amendment must be approved by the maintainers according to the project's
   documented decision process, with consensus preferred and majority decision
   used when consensus is not reached.
4. The amended charter is effective once the change is merged and the version
   history is updated to reflect the new revision.

If a governance change is urgent or required to maintain safety, security, or
project continuity, maintainers may take temporary action while documenting the
decision and following up with a formal governance review.

## Interpretation and Conflict Resolution

If there is uncertainty about how this charter applies to a particular issue or
policy, the maintainers interpret the charter in a manner that best supports the
project mission, transparent governance, and the safety of contributors and users.

When conflicts arise between this charter and a specific governance policy, the
more specific policy governs unless the conflict is material to the charter's
purpose or the policy would undermine the principles stated in this document.
Where necessary, the maintainers may resolve conflicts through the project's
documented escalation and resolution processes.

## Charter Status and Accountability

The maintainers are responsible for keeping this charter current, ensuring it
matches the project's actual governance practices, and updating it as the project
matures. This charter is a foundational governance document and should be read
alongside the other policies in `Governance/`.

The project succeeds when decisions are clear, contributors understand how they can
participate, and maintainers can act consistently and transparently while
preserving the community's trust.

## Summary

TeachLink Mobile is governed by a transparent, maintainer-led model that values
contributor input, procedural fairness, and documented decisions. This charter
provides the foundation for the project's governance and ensures that decisions are
made under a clear and consistent framework.
