# Governance Glossary

This glossary defines key terms used in the governance of TeachLink Mobile.

## Terms

*   **BDFL**: Benevolent Dictator For Life. The person who makes final decisions in the project. (See also: [Maintainer](#maintainer))
*   **Commit**: A saved change to the project's source code.
*   **Contributor**: Anyone who contributes to the project, whether through code, documentation, or other means.
*   **Maintainer**: Individuals who have commit access and are responsible for reviewing and merging contributions. (See also: [BDFL](#bdfl), [Reviewer](#reviewer))
*   **Pull Request (PR)**: A proposed set of changes submitted by a contributor for review before being merged into the main codebase.
*   **Reviewer**: Someone who reviews a pull request. (See also: [Maintainer](#maintainer))
*   **Working Group**: A temporary or permanent group of contributors focused on a specific aspect of the project.
